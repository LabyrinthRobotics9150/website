About Our Team

Founded in 2023, Labyrinth Robotics is a student-led, community-based FRC team driven by the mission to make STEM accessible to youth across our region. As a proud participant in the global FIRST Robotics Competition, we design, build, and program industrial-grade robots to tackle each competition season's challenges while learning real-world engineering, teamwork, and leadership skills.
